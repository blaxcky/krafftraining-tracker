The SVG paths in index.html and js/icons.js were converted from the font bundled
with material-symbols 0.47.5 (https://github.com/marella/material-symbols).
They were instantiated at wght=500, FILL=0, GRAD=0, opsz=24, transformed from
font coordinates to a 0 0 960 960 SVG viewBox, and rounded to three decimals.
Only the 19 icons used by this app are included. No icon font is required.
